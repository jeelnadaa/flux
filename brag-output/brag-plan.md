# Flux v2 launch video: plan

**What it is.** A free audio editor for Windows, macOS and Linux. It puts presets on everything your computer
plays, clones your voice (with consent), and lets an AI agent drive all of it.
**For.** People who want 8D, slowed or radio sound on whatever they hear, creators, and people who use agents.
**Sets it apart.** Live presets on the whole computer's sound and the mic; 162 MCP tools; on-device.
**Hook.** "Your music. Your calls. Your mic." then "Live, in 8D." with a dot orbiting the words.
**Tone.** `cinematic` crossed with `default`: big kinetic type, fluid camera, no hard cuts. Every scene change is
an animated transition (an orbit iris, a playhead wipe, theme bands, a circle close).
**Identity.** The app's Cassette theme (#DCCBA3 ground, #1E1A10 ink, #B8350A signal), Bricolage Grotesque with its
width axis animated (75% to 100%), Geist Mono for labels, the real `f|ux` wordmark with its red playhead bar.
**Real UI.** Screens captured from the real app on the real engine (`work/capture.py`): Live, 3D/8D stage, voice
cloning script, Agents. The song in them is synthetic ("Night Drive", made by the capture script). Device names
shown are hardware models, not personal data.
**Share caption.** Below, in share-copy.txt.

## Music
120 BPM (a beat every 0.5 s, a bar every 2 s), A minor, Am-F-C-G. Kick, hats, sub bass, pad, a pluck arpeggio that
circles left-right in the Live scene (8D, as the video says), soft risers into transitions and low booms in A on
scene changes. Cuts land on beats.
