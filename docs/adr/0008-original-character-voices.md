# ADR-0008: Original Character Voices Instead of Celebrity Impersonations

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Deciders:** Flux Engineering
- **Date:** 2026-11-03
- **Traceability:** PLAN.md §1 (D8), §0 principle 9, §4

## Context
Voice changers and AI synthesis frequently offer presets named after or imitating celebrities, public figures, or trademarked characters. This creates severe legal liability (right of publicity, trademark infringement, biometric privacy legislation, EU AI Act obligations) and ethical risks regarding voice impersonation and fraud.

## Decision
1. Explicitly drop all celebrity-style voice presets, impersonation modes, and trademarked names.
2. Replace them entirely with original, stylized character voices: Robot, Cyborg, Alien, Monster, Announcer, Whisper, Deep, Chipmunk, Child, and Elder.
3. Require explicit user consent attestations for custom voice cloning features, enforce machine-readable synthetic audio metadata tagging, and prohibit unconsented voice imitation in terms of service.

## Consequences
- **Positive:** Complete elimination of publicity right claims and trademark disputes; clear ethical boundaries; simplified enterprise adoption.
- **Negative:** None; original stylized voices provide rich creative utility without legal exposure.
